# Style guide (DRAFT — agent-generated, review before use)

Voice is plain, factual, and informational -- short declarative sentences stating what the product does or offers, with no hedging or hype language in the source material. Formatting favors a direct Q&A structure for FAQ-style content (short question, one- to two-sentence answer) and short descriptive paragraphs for about/overview content, each answer or claim grounded in a specific, checkable detail (a number, a date, a named quantity) rather than vague superlatives.
