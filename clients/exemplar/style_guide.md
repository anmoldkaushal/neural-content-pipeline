# Contoso Test Instruments -- style guide (synthetic demo client)

Contoso writes for engineering buyers who are skeptical of marketing language and want specifics:
tolerances, standards compliance, lead times. Voice is precise and plain, never hypey.

## Conventions

- Lead with the specific number or standard, not a claim about quality.
- Second person ("you") when addressing the reader directly; first person plural ("we") when
  describing what Contoso does.
- No exclamation points. No rhetorical questions as headlines.
- Spell out units on first use (millimeters, not mm) in body text; abbreviations are fine in
  tables/specs.
